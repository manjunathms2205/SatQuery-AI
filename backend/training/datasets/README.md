# Training Datasets for Remote Sensing Adaptation

This directory holds references and configurations for open, labeled Earth observation benchmark datasets:

1. **BigEarthNet-S2**:
   - 590,326 Sentinel-2 L2A patches across Europe.
   - 12 multispectral bands.
   - 19 CORINE Land Cover classes.

2. **EuroSAT**:
   - 27,000 Sentinel-2 patches covering 10 land cover classes (Industrial, Residential, AnnualCrop, PermanentCrop, River, SeaLake, Forest, HerbaceousVegetation, Highway, Pasture).

3. **OSCD (Onera Satellite Change Detection)**:
   - 24 pairs of Sentinel-2 multispectral images for urban and environmental change detection.

> [!NOTE]
> The bundled `Easy-EO` sample dataset (`sentinel2_stacked`) is used strictly for development, pipeline validation, and demonstration, and is **not** a supervised training dataset.
